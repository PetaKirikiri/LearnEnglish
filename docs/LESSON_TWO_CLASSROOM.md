# Lesson 2: Where are the / my / your …?

Canonical teaching page: `/classroom/index.html?lesson=2`.
The older `?lesson=where-are-my` notes URL redirects here.
Use the existing classroom shell, four aligned sentence slots, Thai meanings and pictures. The flag switch changes teaching instructions; English examples and their Thai meanings remain together.

## Teaching through the existing learning windows

Lesson 2 renders the same SentenceLine and WordExplanation components as Lesson 1. It adds vocabulary and the are reference, not a separate teaching interface. There are no extra step tabs, vocabulary screen, or comparison layout.

1. Open the noun box. Use its existing word chooser to introduce shoe, key, sock, bag, book and pen. Every noun has its Thai meaning and picture in that window.
2. Keep the determiner as the. Choose shoe, then shoes. Returning to the sentence shows the same four boxes, with is or are matched to the noun.
3. Open the verb box. Its existing reference window explains are and offers is/are. Changing it also changes the selected noun's form, so the sentence stays grammatical.
4. Open the determiner box and choose my, then your. The existing window explains speaker/listener ownership. Noun number still controls is/are.
5. Add ticket, sandal, boot, shirt, hat, pencil, phone, charger, towel, bottle, cup and plate through that same noun chooser.
6. Teach glasses and trousers in the noun window. One pair still takes are; the is choice is disabled for these nouns. Never teach glass or trouser as their singular forms here.
7. Use the existing Practice navigation for the race. Teach a round's words before selecting it.

## Race sequence

The teacher chooses the next round after teaching its prerequisites. Round changes retain the room, QR code and joined students. Lesson 2 has a separate saved host room from Lesson 1.

| Round | Skill | Scope | Pool |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Correct / incorrect is–are agreement | all 18 regular noun pairs plus glasses/trousers; the/my/your; complete sentences | 228 |
| 2 | Understand my vs your | six core nouns, both numbers; same noun and number in both Thai choices | 24 |
| 3 | English to Thai translation | all Lesson 2 forms with the/my/your, plus the 20 Lesson 1 nouns in their taught singular frame | 134 |

Pool counts are available variants, not a required 386-question session. Round 1 balances correct/incorrect agreement. Round 3 uses only correct English with two Thai location questions; its distractor changes number, owner or object. Thai number contrasts explicitly use one/several with the noun classifier. Pair nouns do not imply multiple pairs. Earlier place vocabulary stays in its familiar singular frame. The server preserves these authored choices and randomizes their answer positions.

Every race question has locally generated recorded speech (228 Lesson 2 recordings plus the 20 existing Lesson 1 recordings). The current classroom briefing shows the selected round’s answer controls and timing; joining stays in its compact QR control. QR targets are public production URLs, including when teaching from localhost.

Do not use round three until its vocabulary and the pair-noun exception have been taught. These are teacher-led transitions, not an automated measurement of student mastery. This lesson does not introduce location prepositions or new tenses.
