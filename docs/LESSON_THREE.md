# Lesson 3 — Where are you?

Classroom: `/classroom/index.html?lesson=3`. Phone notes: `/english/notes?lesson=3`.

Lesson 3 teaches present locations with **I, you, he, she, am, is, are, at**. New words relative to Lessons 1–2: **I, you, he, she, am, at**. Was/were and his/her are no longer active Lesson 3 content.

## Shared matrix carousel

The first slide preserves the original **Where | is / are | [the / my / your | noun]** matrix, its cumulative vocabulary, and its separate determiner and noun education panels. Do not replace it with a person-only question layout.

The second slide adds **I / you / he / she | am / is / are | at | [the | place]**. The final yellow unit is a noun phrase with two separately clickable pieces, not one word called "the hotel". The final two pieces use the shared noun-group renderer. The four top-level units therefore contain five individual words/pieces.

The existing Ask / Answer buttons and carousel arrows select these slides. Person selection in the answer pairs I/am, you/are, he/is and she/is. A location noun does not determine the answer's verb. "The" identifies a known referent; it does not express singularity. Each piece keeps its own contextual meaning and pronunciation view.

The person questions (Where are you? / Where is he? / Where am I?) remain in the Lesson 3 practice bank; they do not replace the original teaching slide.

## Places and practice

The cumulative noun catalogue is retained. The new answer picker selects the nine singular destinations suited to **at the …**: hotel, toilet, gym, padel court, clinic, school, bank, bookstore, restaurant. It excludes objects and reserves bedroom for a later **in** contrast. Earlier lessons and their vocabulary pickers are unchanged.

The practice sequence has 84 questions: 12 person/agreement judgements, 36 English-to-Thai location answers, and 36 question/answer exchanges. Answer-stage distractors keep the destination identical and change the person; dialogue review changes the destination. Correct and deliberately incorrect judgement prompts have matching recordings.

## Audio and education

Every word's education panel includes the shared, open pronunciation component. The new structural words have editable English and Thai sound-spelling sketches. These are teaching sketches, not measured pitch contours.
