# Lesson 3 — Where, now and before

Open `/classroom/index.html?lesson=3`, or select 03 in the lesson rail.

Use the same shared sentence matrix and education sections. The question stays **Where**. Slot 2 offers **is / are / was / were**; slot 3 offers **the / my / your / his / her**. Noun changes preserve tense and ownership while updating agreement. One clock represents a singular noun and two clocks a plural noun; the backward arrow marks past time. Earlier lessons retain their earlier grammar choices.

## Teaching sequence

1. Recall familiar present-tense examples with phone/phones, shoe/shoes, keys and bags.
2. Show **was** with one familiar object, then **were** with several. Contrast now with earlier; was/were do not mean a specific day.
3. Introduce **his**, then **her**, with familiar nouns and present tense. These identify the person whose thing it is; the noun's number chooses the verb.
4. Introduce the new nouns with their pictures, then combine the familiar pieces.
5. Practise: past agreement, ownership meaning, mixed review. The first two pools isolate the new contrasts; mixed review returns to the whole cumulative vocabulary. Thai choices keep the same noun and vary ownership or time, so matching the noun alone cannot answer them.
